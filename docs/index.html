Who does what

Only a Registered Training Organisation (RTO) can issue a nationally recognised qualification. Here’s how the work is shared.

Poonam at Pathwise

  • Helps you choose the right course and pathway
  • Checks your eligibility and explains the costs
  • Guides you through enrolment and organising evidence
  • Checks in and answers questions along the way

The partner RTO ● To confirm: Add partner RTO names and RTO codes

  • Enrols you and provides the training
  • Assesses your skills and evidence
  • Arranges work placement requirements
  • Issues your qualification and transcript

You’ll always know which RTO you’re enrolling with before you pay anything. You can look up any course or RTO on training.gov.au.

Questions

Good questions to ask first

I’m not sure which course is right for me. Where do I start?

Start with a free chat or the online eligibility check. Tell Poonam about your work so far and where you want to go, and she will suggest a course and the most sensible way to get it – or tell you honestly if now isn’t the right time.

Is RPL a shortcut or a “bought” certificate?

No. You still have to prove competence with real evidence, and an assessor will usually talk with you and may ask you to demonstrate tasks. If there are gaps, you complete extra training or assessment. The qualification you receive is the same one people get through full study.

Who actually delivers the course and issues my certificate?

Nationally recognised qualifications can only be issued by a Registered Training Organisation (RTO). Pathwise Courses is an advisory service, not an RTO: Poonam helps you choose and prepare, and a partner RTO enrols, trains, assesses and issues your qualification. You will always be told which RTO before you enrol. ● To confirm: Check this answer matches how Poonam works

How much do courses cost?

Fees depend on the course, the RTO and whether you study the full course or use RPL. Poonam will give you the full cost in writing – including any extra fees – before you decide. ● To confirm: Check this answer matches how Poonam works
