My story

Why I do this

Choosing a course can feel overwhelming. There are hundreds of providers, unfamiliar codes and plenty of sales pressure. I started Pathwise Courses to make that simpler.

Many of the people I speak with already do skilled work in aged care, disability support, childcare and other fields, but don’t yet hold the qualification to prove it. Others are starting fresh and want to know the quickest sensible route into a new career. Either way, my job is to help you make a good decision – and then make the process as smooth as possible.

What I do

How I can help

  • Course advice

    Talk through your goals and find a qualification that fits your experience, your timeline and the jobs you want.

  • Free eligibility checks

    Find out whether you meet the entry requirements – and whether RPL is realistic – before you spend anything.

  • RPL evidence support

    Work out which documents to gather and how to organise them so your assessor can see your skills clearly.

  • Enrolment guidance

    Understand the paperwork, the costs and the timeline, and get set up with the right training organisation.

  • Check-ins along the way

    A familiar person to ask when you’re stuck, unsure what’s next, or need a nudge to keep going.

  • Training for teams

    Short courses and qualification pathways for employers who want to upskill their staff.

Your first conversation

What to expect when we talk

Our first chat usually takes about 15 minutes, by phone, WhatsApp or video – whichever you prefer.

  • I’ll ask about your work history, existing qualifications and goals
  • We’ll look at the courses and pathways that suit you
  • You’ll get a clear idea of time, costs and next steps
  • There’s no obligation and no payment details needed

Handy to have nearby

  • Your resume or a list of past roles
  • Any certificates or qualifications you hold
  • The course or job you have in mind (if you know)
  • Questions about time, cost or placement
