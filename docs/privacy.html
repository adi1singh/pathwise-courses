Privacy

Privacy notice

How we collect, use and look after the personal information you share through this website.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry or complete the eligibility check, we collect the details you enter – such as your name, email, phone number, state, the course you’re interested in, and information about your work experience and qualifications.

Why we collect it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, assess which courses and pathways may suit you, and help you enrol if you choose to. We won’t add you to a marketing list without your permission.

Who we share it with

With your agreement, we share relevant details with the Registered Training Organisation (RTO) you decide to enrol with, so they can process your enrolment. Our website form service stores submissions on our behalf. We don’t sell your information.

How we look after it

We keep your information only as long as we need it to help you, and take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Contact Poonam at [email address].

This website

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The pathway check and RPL evidence checklist run entirely in your browser; the evidence checklist remembers your ticks on your own device only.
