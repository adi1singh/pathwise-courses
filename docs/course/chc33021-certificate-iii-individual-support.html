About this course

This certificate teaches you how to support people with everyday living, personal care and wellbeing, safely and respectfully. It is the qualification most aged care and disability employers look for when hiring support workers.

Is it right for you?

This course is a good fit for:

  • People starting a career in aged care, disability or home care
  • Support workers who have the experience but not the certificate
  • Carers who want their skills formally recognised

Jobs it can lead to

  • Personal care worker
  • Aged care worker
  • Disability support worker
  • Home and community care worker
  • Respite care worker

Job titles vary between employers and states. Some roles also need experience or extra checks.

What you’ll cover

  • Person-centred support and individual rights
  • Personal care, mobility and daily living support
  • Recognising and responding to health changes
  • Safe work practices and infection control
  • Communication, culture and inclusion
  • Specialist electives such as dementia, disability or palliative care

The exact units depend on the electives the training provider offers. The full list of units for CHC33021 is on training.gov.au .

Ways to get this qualification

Recognition of prior learning (RPL)

For people with relevant experience. Show what you already know, then fill any gaps. Typical timeframe: Often 4–12 weeks, depending on your evidence.

How RPL works

Full course, studied online

Learn the full course at your own pace with trainer support. Typical timeframe: 6–12 months.

How online study works

Work placement ● To confirm: Typical figure – confirm with the delivering RTO

At least 120 hours in an approved workplace. If you already work in the industry, your current workplace may be able to host your placement.

What you’ll need ● To confirm: Typical figure – confirm with the delivering RTO

  • A Unique Student Identifier (USI) – free to create at usi.gov.au
  • Language, literacy and numeracy skills suited to the course (usually a short check)
  • Access to a computer or tablet and reliable internet
  • A National Police Check (and NDIS Worker Screening for disability work)

Entry requirements are set by the training provider. Poonam will check them with you before you enrol.

Common questions

Can I get the Certificate III in Individual Support through RPL?

If you already have relevant work experience, you may be able to have it assessed through recognition of prior learning. You would provide evidence of your skills and talk with an assessor, then complete any gap training needed. Poonam can tell you whether RPL is realistic for you after a short chat.

Will I need to do a work placement?

Yes. At least 120 hours in an approved workplace. If you already work in a suitable role, some or all of it may be completed in your current workplace. ● To confirm: Check this answer matches how Poonam works

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the training provider and whether you use RPL or study the full course. Poonam will give you the full cost in writing before you decide – just ask. ● To confirm: Check this answer matches how Poonam works

Is this course nationally recognised?

Yes. CHC33021 is part of Australia’s national training system. You can look it up on training.gov.au.
