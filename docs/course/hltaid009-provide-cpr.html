About this course

This nationally recognised unit teaches you to recognise an unresponsive casualty, perform CPR on adults, children and infants, and use an automated external defibrillator (AED). Many workplaces ask staff to renew it each year.

Who it suits

  • Care, education and fitness workers who need a current CPR certificate
  • Anyone renewing their yearly CPR
  • Parents and community volunteers

Often required for

  • Required or recommended for most care, childcare, school and community roles

Study options and commitment ● To confirm: Typical figure – confirm with the delivering RTO

Online learning + short practical session

Complete the theory online, then a short in-person session where an assessor watches you perform the practical skills.

Practical assessment

A practical assessment, including CPR on a manikin on the floor, is required.

Entry requirements ● To confirm: Typical figure – confirm with the delivering RTO

  • A Unique Student Identifier (USI)
  • Ability to kneel on the floor and perform two minutes of uninterrupted CPR

Entry requirements are set by the training provider. Poonam checks them with you before you enrol.

What you’ll cover

  • Checking for danger and response (DRSABCD)
  • CPR on adults, children and infants
  • Using an AED safely
  • Managing the casualty until help arrives
  • Reporting the incident
