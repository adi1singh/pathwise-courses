About this course

This is the first aid course most employers mean when they ask for a “first aid certificate”. It covers CPR plus how to manage common injuries and illnesses until medical help arrives.

Is it right for you?

This course is a good fit for:

  • Workers who need a first aid certificate for their job
  • Childcare and care workers (check if your role needs a specialist unit)
  • Anyone who wants to be ready in an emergency

Who needs it

  • Required for workplace first aiders and many care, education and trade roles

What you’ll cover

  • CPR and using an AED
  • Bleeding, burns, fractures and wounds
  • Asthma, anaphylaxis and allergic reactions
  • Choking, seizures and medical emergencies
  • Communicating in an emergency and reporting

The exact units depend on the electives the training provider offers. The full list of units for HLTAID011 is on training.gov.au .

Ways to get this certificate

Online learning + short practical session

Complete the theory online, then a short in-person session where an assessor watches you perform the practical skills.

Practical assessment

A practical assessment, including CPR on a manikin on the floor, is required.

What you’ll need ● To confirm: Typical figure – confirm with the delivering RTO

  • A Unique Student Identifier (USI)
  • Ability to kneel on the floor and perform two minutes of uninterrupted CPR

Entry requirements are set by the training provider. Poonam will check them with you before you enrol.
