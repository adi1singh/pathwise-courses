About this course

The diploma prepares you to manage cases, run programs and supervise staff in community organisations – from housing and family services to disability and youth work. It is a common requirement for coordinator and case manager positions.

Is it right for you?

This course is a good fit for:

  • Experienced community, disability or aged care workers
  • Case workers who want a formal leadership qualification
  • Certificate IV graduates aiming for coordinator roles

Jobs it can lead to

  • Case manager
  • Community services coordinator
  • Program or project officer
  • Family support worker
  • Team leader – community services

Job titles vary between employers and states. Some roles also need experience or extra checks.

What you’ll cover

  • Case management and complex needs
  • Advocacy and client rights
  • Program planning and evaluation
  • Supervising and supporting staff
  • Working with diverse communities
  • Ethical and legal practice

The exact units depend on the electives the training provider offers. The full list of units for CHC52025 is on training.gov.au .

Ways to get this qualification

For people with relevant experience. Show what you already know, then fill any gaps. Typical timeframe: Often 6–12 weeks, depending on your evidence.

Common questions

Can I get the Diploma of Community Services through RPL?

If you already have relevant work experience, you may be able to have it assessed through recognition of prior learning. You would provide evidence of your skills and talk with an assessor, then complete any gap training needed. Poonam can tell you whether RPL is realistic for you after a short chat.

Will I need to do a work placement?

Yes. Required – hours set by the qualification. If you already work in a suitable role, some or all of it may be completed in your current workplace. ● To confirm: Check this answer matches how Poonam works

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the training provider and whether you use RPL or study the full course. Poonam will give you the full cost in writing before you decide – just ask. ● To confirm: Check this answer matches how Poonam works

Is this course nationally recognised?

Yes. CHC52025 is part of Australia’s national training system. You can look it up on training.gov.au.
