About this course

This certificate covers the practical side of workplace health and safety: managing risks, consulting with workers, responding to incidents and meeting legal duties. It suits WHS officers and supervisors in any industry.

Who it suits

  • Supervisors and team leaders with safety responsibilities
  • Health and safety representatives
  • People moving into WHS officer roles

Jobs it can lead to

  • WHS officer or coordinator
  • Safety advisor
  • Site or operations supervisor
  • Health and safety representative

Job titles vary between employers and states. Some roles also need experience or extra checks.

Study options and commitment

Recognition of prior learning (RPL)

Typically 4–10 weeks

For people with relevant experience. You show what you already know, then fill any gaps. Timing depends mostly on how quickly you gather evidence.

How RPL works

Full course, studied online

Typically 6–12 months

Learn the full course at your own pace with trainer support. Most people studying alongside work plan for around 8–15 hours a week.

How online study works

Work placement

No placement – assessed through workplace tasks and projects.

Entry requirements

  • A Unique Student Identifier (USI) – free to create at usi.gov.au

Common questions

Can I get the Certificate IV in Work Health and Safety through RPL?

If you already have relevant work experience, you may be able to have it assessed through recognition of prior learning. You would provide evidence of your skills and talk with an assessor, then complete any gap training needed. Poonam can tell you whether RPL is realistic for you after a short chat.

Is there a work placement?

No placement – assessed through workplace tasks and projects.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the training provider and whether you use RPL or study the full course. Poonam will give you the full cost in writing before you decide – just ask.

Is this course nationally recognised?

Yes. BSB41419 is part of Australia’s national training system. You can look it up on training.gov.au.
