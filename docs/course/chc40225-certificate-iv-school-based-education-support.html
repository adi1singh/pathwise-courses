About this course

This qualification prepares you to support teachers and students with literacy, numeracy, behaviour and inclusion. It suits teacher aides, learning support officers and integration aides.

Who it suits

  • Teacher aides and learning support officers
  • Parents and volunteers who already help in schools
  • People moving from childcare into school settings

Jobs it can lead to

  • Education support officer
  • Teacher aide or assistant
  • Integration or inclusion aide
  • Learning support officer

Job titles vary between employers and states. Some roles also need experience or extra checks.

Study options and commitment

Recognition of prior learning (RPL)

Typically 4–12 weeks

For people with relevant experience. You show what you already know, then fill any gaps. Timing depends mostly on how quickly you gather evidence.

How RPL works

Full course, studied online

Typically 6–12 months

Learn the full course at your own pace with trainer support. Most people studying alongside work plan for around 8–15 hours a week.

How online study works

Work placement

At least 100 hours in a school setting. If you already work in the industry, your current workplace may be able to host your placement.

Entry requirements

  • A Unique Student Identifier (USI) – free to create at usi.gov.au
  • Language, literacy and numeracy skills suited to the course (usually a short check)
  • Access to a computer or tablet and reliable internet
  • A valid Working with Children Check

Entry requirements are set by the training provider. Poonam checks them with you before you enrol.

Common questions

Can I get the Certificate IV in School Based Education Support through RPL?

If you already have relevant work experience, you may be able to have it assessed through recognition of prior learning. You would provide evidence of your skills and talk with an assessor, then complete any gap training needed. Poonam can tell you whether RPL is realistic for you after a short chat.

Will I need to do a work placement?

Yes. At least 100 hours in a school setting. If you already work in a suitable role, some or all of it may be completed in your current workplace.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the training provider and whether you use RPL or study the full course. Poonam will give you the full cost in writing before you decide – just ask.

Is this course nationally recognised?

Yes. CHC40225 is part of Australia’s national training system. You can look it up on training.gov.au.
