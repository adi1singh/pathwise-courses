Recognition of prior learning

Your experience counts

RPL is a formal way of having the skills you already use at work assessed against a nationally recognised qualification.

If you’ve worked in care, education, training, building or management – paid or unpaid, here or overseas – you may already meet many of the requirements. An assessor from the training organisation reviews your evidence, talks with you about your work, and identifies any gaps. You then complete only what’s missing.

RPL isn’t a shortcut. You still have to prove you’re competent, and you receive exactly the same qualification as someone who studied the full course.

RPL could suit you if you…

  • Have worked in the industry for a year or more
  • Do the job well but don’t hold the qualification
  • Gained skills or qualifications overseas
  • Need a qualification for a promotion, registration or new role
  • Want to avoid repeating what you already know

How RPL works

  1. 1

    Free chat and eligibility check

    Poonam asks about your experience and suggests the qualification that best matches it.

  2. 2

    Self-assessment

    You complete a checklist comparing your skills with the units in the qualification.

  3. 3

    Build your evidence

    You gather documents that show your skills – Poonam helps you organise them.

  4. 4

    Talk with an assessor

    The RTO’s assessor discusses your work with you and may ask you to demonstrate tasks.

  5. 5

    Fill any gaps

    If anything is missing, you complete targeted training or extra assessment.

  6. 6

    Receive your qualification

    Once you’re assessed as competent in every unit, the RTO issues your qualification.

Full course online

Learn everything, at your own pace

If you’re new to the field, studying the full course gives you the knowledge and practice you need – without sitting in a classroom.

You work through learning materials and assessments on the training organisation’s online platform, with a trainer you can contact for help. Many care and education courses include a work placement, so you practise your skills in a real workplace under supervision.

  • Study on any devicePhone, tablet or computer – whenever suits you.
  • Trainer supportHelp by phone, email or video when you’re stuck.
  • Your paceMost people plan for 8–15 hours a week alongside work. ● To confirm: Typical weekly hours – confirm
  • Real-world placementRequired for many care and education courses.

How online study works

  1. 1

    Choose and enrol

    Poonam helps you pick a course; you enrol with the training organisation and receive your login.

  2. 2

    Learn online

    Work through each unit’s readings, videos and activities in order, at a pace that suits you.

  3. 3

    Complete assessments

    Answer questions, complete case studies and projects, and get feedback from your trainer.

  4. 4

    Work placement

    If your course needs one, put your skills into practice in a real workplace.

  5. 5

    Get qualified

    When every unit is complete, the RTO issues your qualification and transcript.
